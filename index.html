Setlo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: Setlo does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Setlo stores the information you enter — workouts, sets, reps, weights, workout templates, body weight, training goals, and app settings — locally on your iPhone. This data is not sent to us or to any third party.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Setlo schedules local notifications on your device (for example, when a rest timer ends). They are created on the device and are not sent through any server.

Purchases

The current version contains no in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Setlo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting data (for example, optional cloud sync), this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/VladislavSournine/setlo-privacy.
